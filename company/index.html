The company · Since 17 September 1982

Founded on Bonaire.Grown with our clients.

Ship and liner agency, cargo handling, freight consolidation, customs brokerage and moving. One company across four islands.

History

Clients asked usto follow them.

The expansion to Curaçao and Aruba was not a plan on paper. Major clients in the oil and shipping industries asked for the same service they had on Bonaire. So we opened our own offices next to their refineries, and later on St. Eustatius.

  1. 1982

    Founded on Bonaire

    Rocargo Services opens on 17 September 1982 as a ship and liner agency in Kralendijk.

  2. 1994

    Curaçao and Aruba

    At the request of major clients, Rocargo starts serving the oil and shipping industries on Curaçao and Aruba.

  3. 1995

    New headquarters

    Office and warehouse move to Kaya Industria, close to customs, the port and the banks.

  4. 1999

    Curaçao office

    Our own office opens in September, close to the Isla refinery.

  5. 2001

    Aruba office

    Our own office opens in May, near the San Nicolas refinery.

  6. 2014

    St. Eustatius

    The newest branch opens for principals calling at Statia.

Mission

To provide ship owners, charterers, cargo receivers, shippers, manning agents and all individual clients and companies connected to maritime activities with a reliable, efficient and comprehensive partner to take care of their particular needs at the ports where we operate and beyond.

Vision

To be flexible and continue modernizing in order to meet the ever changing requirements of the international maritime markets and by doing so becoming the leading agents in the areas where we operate.
